W-01 · Trade-area figures

City and county proxies replaced with Census-tract evidence, with margins of error shown on every estimate.

Why this page exists

A restaurant should be evaluated against a parcel-centred trade area, not city or county averages. The exact parcel location is intentionally not published. Earlier ACS values taken from a third-party mirror have been withdrawn pending direct official verification; the tables below therefore leave those values blank rather than presenting unconfirmed neighbourhood evidence.

What this means for the decision

No income, rent, poverty, population, housing-tenure, or school-related conclusion about the immediate neighbourhood is supported yet. Do not use the withdrawn values to justify either a renewal rent or a sale premium. The central-LA reference coordinate is not the parcel.

What is still missing before this becomes a rent figure

The secure workflow must select consistent Census tracts from the parcel location, verify official ACS estimates and margins of error, and then combine them with official traffic counts, planning evidence, school attendance areas, and competitor research. A demographic measure is not a rent comparable: short-lease pricing still requires written broker/appraisal quotes and actual lease terms, kept in the private adviser workspace.

Suggested improvements

  • Run the tract lookup at the parcel in the secure adviser workflow. Keep the address and selected tract IDs offline; publish only aggregate non-identifying results.
  • Pull the 2020–2024 ACS tables directly from Census. Record the table, geography, vintage, estimate, margin of error, and retrieval date; no mirror substitutions.
  • Compare multiple consistent tracts only after direct verification. Document why each tract belongs in the drive-time catchment and distinguish observations from assumptions.
  • Add official traffic counts and local planning evidence. Label gaps where an agency does not publish the data; obtain competitor and rent evidence privately from qualified advisers.

Privacy. This site publishes only aggregate, non-identifying findings. No owner name, tenant contact, lease document, parcel identifier, loan figure or financial record appears here. Those belong in the owner's access-controlled internal workspace with authorised advisers.

Nature of this site. It is a research and decision-gate register. It is not an appraisal, a tax opinion, a legal opinion, or investment advice. A blank means "not verified", not "zero".
