Findings

Schools

District-level evidence that was verified, and a candid record of the school-quality data that was not.

An honest gap

School quality is one of the factors the owner asked to be examined, and it is one of the two where this build could not obtain an official figure. The California Department of Education's assessment results and the California School Dashboard were both unreachable, and the NCES school locator is JavaScript-driven. Rather than substitute a third-party rating, this page records what was verified and names exactly what is missing.

What was verified

MeasureValuePeriodSource
Los Angeles Unified School District enrolment (NCES LEAID 0622710)408,026 students2024–2025S27

LAUSD is the district serving central Los Angeles, and one of the largest in the United States by enrolment. That tells you the district is enormous; it tells you nothing about the school at the end of the street.

A note on the district count that was discarded

The NCES-derived endpoint returned a county filter that did not take effect: the response contained Alameda County districts and a count matching all of California rather than Los Angeles County. Rather than report a number that was quietly wrong, the figure was discarded and recorded as unverified. This is the kind of error that stays invisible if you only read the output and never check the rows.

What this does to the decision

It does not block it. School quality is a second-order factor for a roadside quick-service restaurant whose customers are drawn overwhelmingly from a drive-time catchment rather than a school catchment. It is recorded as an open gate at "nice-to-have" priority so that it is not lost, but the sell-or-renew decision does not turn on it. The gates that do turn on it — value, basis, lease, rent — are all on the Decision gates board.

Suggested improvements

  • Use the California School Dashboard's downloadable data files rather than scraping the interactive page; the files are the official machine-readable source and were not tried here.
  • Resolve attendance areas from the LAUSD address lookup once the parcel address exists, and cite the specific schools by name.
  • Do not substitute GreatSchools or Niche ratings for the state's own published results, however convenient they are to scrape.
