Suggested improvements

  • Retrieve official ACS values directly from Census with a valid API key or a table-level data.census.gov review; retain table IDs, estimate, margin of error, geography, vintage, and retrieval date.
  • Build a versioned tract trend series only after the direct pull succeeds. Do not join inconsistent geographies or treat a five-year ACS average as a one-year observation.
  • Use California DOF projections separately from ACS observations. Label projections and their baseline/vintage clearly.

Privacy. This site publishes only aggregate, non-identifying findings. No owner name, tenant contact, lease document, parcel identifier, loan figure or financial record appears here. Those belong in the owner's access-controlled internal workspace with authorised advisers.
