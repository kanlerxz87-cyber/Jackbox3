Risk register

Every decision mapped to the risks that threaten it, with black-swan scenarios and the tripwires that would prompt a reconsideration.

Risks by decision, not by category

A risk list is only useful if it is attached to a choice. Below, each risk is mapped to the decision it threatens, scored on likelihood and consequence using the evidence in this repository, and paired with the specific action that reduces it. The scores are this project's judgement and are labelled as such — they are not measurements.

How to think about the balance

The owner asked for a decision that preserves wealth while keeping opportunity for growth at a manageable level of risk. The verified evidence points to a specific asymmetry worth naming: the downside scenarios here are liquidity events, not gradual-erosion events. An earthquake, an insurer withdrawal or a tenant bankruptcy all act by removing the owner's ability to choose. A gradual decline in the trade area, by contrast, is visible in the data years ahead and can be responded to.

That asymmetry is the strongest argument in this entire workspace, and it is an argument about sequencing and reserves rather than about sell-versus-renew. Close the information gates, size the reserve so that a liquidity event is survivable without a forced sale, and the sell-or-renew question becomes a genuine choice instead of a response to pressure.

Suggested improvements

  • Convert the qualitative likelihood scores into a formal matrix once a value and a rent exist, so the risk can be expressed in dollars rather than adjectives.
  • Add a monitoring dashboard for the four early indicators above, refreshed quarterly.
  • Set explicit tripwires — a named trigger for each risk at which the decision is revisited, rather than waiting for an annual review.

Privacy. This site publishes only aggregate, non-identifying findings. No owner name, tenant contact, lease document, parcel identifier, loan figure or financial record appears here. Those belong in the owner's access-controlled internal workspace with authorised advisers.
