Suggested next actions, in order

  1. Pull the lease and abstract it privately, then reconcile every date to the notice calendar. This informs GATE-01 and GATE-08 at once and is the cheapest high-value action available.
  2. Request the depreciation schedules from the CPA and the closing statement from acquisition. That closes GATE-03.
  3. Request the lender statement and loan agreement. That closes GATE-04, and it may reveal that the loan — not the lease — sets the deadline.
  4. Order the appraisal and the structural report in parallel. Those close GATE-02 and GATE-06 and are the two items that cannot be produced internally.
  5. Get a broker's rent opinion with named comparables. That closes GATE-05 and is what makes the renewal case real.
  6. Only then run the comparison and set a reserve price.
