Methodology &amp; privacy

The verification rules, the privacy model, and an explicit statement of what this site is not.

The rules this build follows

  1. A value is present only if an official source produced it. Otherwise the cell is blank and flagged.
  2. Nothing is inferred, interpolated or filled in. A null from the source stays a null here.
  3. Arithmetic is allowed and labelled. If two verified numbers are divided, the row is marked derived and the formula is recorded.
  4. Observations and assumptions never share a column. Assumptions live only in the private calculator, typed by the reader.
  5. A proxy is called a proxy. The reference point is not the parcel, and every page that uses it says so.
  6. A gap is a finding. "We could not verify this, and here is the document that would" is a real answer.

The privacy model

Personal privacy is the highest-priority constraint in this project, and the design choice that satisfies it is non-collection. This build did not research, record or transmit any of the following:

  • The owner's name, address, contact details, tax identifiers or net worth.
  • The franchisee's or guarantor's identity and financial statements.
  • The lease document, its contents, or any identifying term.
  • The parcel address, assessor parcel number, or exact coordinates.
  • Mortgage balance, lender, rate, or any account number.
  • Insurance policy numbers, premiums or coverage amounts.
  • The tenant's store-level sales, which are not public.

Where a conclusion requires one of these, the site names the document type and the role that holds it (GATE-01 through GATE-20) and stops there. It never paraphrases, approximates or summarises the private contents, because a summary of private data is still private data.

What is published, and why that is safe

Everything on this site is one of three things: a public aggregate (a Census tract statistic covering 2,598 anonymous residents), a public legal or tax rule (an IRS bracket), or a public company filing (a revenue figure from an SEC 10-Q). None of the three identifies a person, and none becomes identifying when combined with the others, because none of them is specific to this property.

The calculator

The private calculator is a single HTML page with one embedded script. It makes no network requests: no fetch, no XMLHttpRequest, no analytics, no third-party fonts, no cookies, no localStorage. Everything you type stays in the browser tab's memory and disappears when you close it. This can be verified by reading assets/calculator.js — it is the only script that page loads — or by watching the browser's network panel while using the form.

What this site is not

  • Not an appraisal. No value opinion is expressed.
  • Not a tax opinion. The calculator applies published brackets to the reader's own numbers.
  • Not investment, legal or accounting advice.
  • Not a projection. No forward-looking figure is published, because the inputs for one are not verified.
  • Not a recommendation to buy, sell, hold or renew. It is a register of what must be known first.

Reproducing this build

Everything is static and generated. python3 build.py regenerates every page from data/findings.json and data/rules.json; python3 scripts/check_site.py validates that every table row carries a source and that no internal link is broken. The verification date is stamped on every page as 2026-11-26.

Privacy. This site publishes only aggregate, non-identifying findings. No owner name, tenant contact, lease document, parcel identifier, loan figure or financial record appears here. Those belong in the owner's access-controlled internal workspace with authorised advisers.

Nature of this site. It is a research and decision-gate register. It is not an appraisal, a tax opinion, a legal opinion, or investment advice. A blank means "not verified", not "zero".
