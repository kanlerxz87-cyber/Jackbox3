Overview

Dashboard

A verified-evidence workspace for one decision: sell before the lease expires, or renew. Every figure traces to an official source; every gap is named rather than filled.

What this workspace is for

The owner holds a Jack in the Box building under a triple-net lease with a stated expiry somewhere between 2030 and 2035. The choice is to sell before expiry or renew. This workspace does not choose for them. It builds both cases side by side, records what is actually known from official sources, and names every gap that has to be closed first.

The honest position today: the single most important number — what the building is worth — is not verified. So is the lease's exact expiry, and every rent, basis and premium figure. No winner is selected here, and none can responsibly be selected, until those exist.

Privacy model, in one paragraph

Personal information is the highest-priority constraint here and it is handled by non-collection, not by redaction. This project never researched the owner, the franchisee, the landlord entity, the guarantors, the loan, or the parcel identifier. The property is represented only by an anonymous reference coordinate in the Los Angeles Civic Center, and every figure on this site is either a public aggregate or a rule published by a tax authority. Where a decision needs private data, this site names the document that contains it and then stops — it does not paraphrase, summarise or approximate the private contents. The calculator runs entirely inside your browser and sends nothing anywhere. See Methodology & privacy.

How the status labels work

LabelMeans
VerifiedRead directly from an official government, state GIS or SEC source during this build. The source link is on the row.
DerivedArithmetic this project performed on two or more verified values. The formula is recorded on the row.
FlaggedFrom an official source, but with a material caveat — a wide margin of error, a vintage mismatch, or a null returned by the source. Read the note.
Not verifiedNo official source could be retrieved. The value cell is blank on purpose. It has not been estimated or filled in.
AssumptionA modelling assumption supplied by the reader in the private calculator. Never presented as data.

Privacy. This site publishes only aggregate, non-identifying findings. No owner name, tenant contact, lease document, parcel identifier, loan figure or financial record appears here. Those belong in the owner's access-controlled internal workspace with authorised advisers.

Nature of this site. It is a research and decision-gate register. It is not an appraisal, a tax opinion, a legal opinion, or investment advice. A blank means "not verified", not "zero".
