Privacy: this public site contains no parcel identifiers, owner names, lease files, addresses, or owner financial data. All subject-property fields are redacted placeholders; only aggregate official statistics and anonymised findings are published. See Privacy & Data Handling.

Private, browser-only calculator

This calculator runs entirely in the reader's browser. It makes no network requests, uses no cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage, and transmits nothing. All owner-specific fields are blank by default; only tax rates that were verified from official sources are pre-filled. Results are assumptions, not official values.
