Subject: a Jack in the Box NNN property described as being around central Los Angeles, with an owner-supplied, unverified lease-expiry range of 2030-2035. The question is whether to sell before expiry or renew, based on local economic activity — while preserving wealth, retaining growth opportunity, and keeping risk manageable.

Best supportable decision today: preserve optionality. Do not commit to a sale or a renewal until decision gates G1-G8 are closed with verified inputs. This site prepares the after-tax sale comparison alongside a negotiated renewal case and deliberately selects no winner yet.
