Suggestions

Twelve concrete improvements, ranked by impact per unit of effort, for the owner to review and shortlist before anything is implemented.

How to use this page

Twelve suggestions, each with the reason it matters, the effort it takes and the concrete first step. They are ordered by a simple rule: things that are cheap, blocking and owner-held come first, because they change the decision most per dollar and per week. Use the checkboxes to shortlist a path; nothing is stored or transmitted.

Suggestion review

Impact is this project's judgement of how much the item changes the sell-versus-renew answer. Effort is an estimate of analyst and adviser time. Select the ones you want to pursue, then take the shortlist to your advisers.

If you only do four things

  1. S-01 — pull the lease, the depreciation schedules and the lender statement, and write down the objective. Zero cost, and it unlocks the analysis.
  2. S-02 — order the appraisal and the structural report. The only two things that cannot be produced in-house.
  3. S-07 — get a rent opinion with named comparables, so the renewal case stops being hypothetical.
  4. S-11 — have a CPA review the tax rules before any number from this site is used in a commitment.

What the site should do next, as a product

  • A single decision-packet export that prints the open gates, the two cases and the verified evidence on four pages for an adviser meeting. The one-page summary is the start of this.
  • A gate tracker that persists locally in the owner's browser, so progress can be recorded without any server.
  • An explicit assumptions register, so that every number used in a future model is dated and attributable.
  • A quarterly refresh job that re-pulls BLS, SEC and FEMA data and flags any material change since the last build.

Privacy. This site publishes only aggregate, non-identifying findings. No owner name, tenant contact, lease document, parcel identifier, loan figure or financial record appears here. Those belong in the owner's access-controlled internal workspace with authorised advisers.

Nature of this site. It is a research and decision-gate register. It is not an appraisal, a tax opinion, a legal opinion, or investment advice. A blank means "not verified", not "zero".
