Property decision — advisor brief

Prepared 2026-11-26 · central Los Angeles · net-leased quick-service restaurant · public sources only · no personal or identifying information is contained in this brief

The decision

Sell the property before the lease expires, or renew. The lease is understood to expire somewhere between 2030 and 2035; the exact date is unverified. No recommendation is made in this brief, because the three inputs that determine the answer are not yet available.

The three missing inputs

  1. Market value — an appraisal plus closed comparables.
  2. Adjusted basis — original cost, improvements, depreciation taken.
  3. Lease terms and market rent — the lease, abstracted, plus a rent opinion with named comparables.

What is established

  • Metro payrolls flat (−0.1% in 2025); LA County SA unemployment 4.6% (Oct 2026p); NAICS 722513 QSR jobs 193,200 (+1.6% YoY); $802.0B county real GDP.
  • AB 1228 fast-food minimum wage $20.00/hr ($16.90 statewide) squeezes stores in CA (41.3% of tenant footprint).
  • Tenant Q3 FY26 SSS −1.1% (−4.2% 40-wk YTD); unit count 2,168 → 2,115 (−53 net); franchise variable rent −18.0% YTD; FY25 net loss $80.7m.
  • Securitized debt $1,492.0m (4.14%–7.62%, $23.3m cash sweep) with ARDs in Aug 2029, May 2031, Feb 2032 inside the 2030–2035 lease window; Note 5 standard leases allow 1–20 yr tenant sole-discretion options.
  • Reference location: FEMA flood Zone X; Design Category D (SDS 1.311 g); USGS 30-yr EQ odds 60% M≥6.7 / 46% M≥7.0; FEMA NRI Very High / community resilience Very Low; FAIR Plan commercial cap ($20M/$100M) sunsets Jan 2028.
  • Tax & transfer friction: Prop 13 avg LA County rate 1.181%; FTB Form 593 3⅓% / 12.3%; LA Measure ULA post-June 2026 4.0% (>$5.4M) / 5.5% (≥$10.9M) + 0.56% base.
  • Tract income 87.1% of county; tract rent 161% of county; poverty 29.4%; QuickFacts/DOF county pop mildly contracting vs SCAG 2050 growth plan.

Risks, in order

  1. Selling without basis, pay-off and a cap rate — selling blind.
  2. Lease expiry ambiguity: 2030 and 2035 are different decisions.
  3. Re-tenanting risk in a shrinking franchise system.
  4. Seismic loss, and the rent interruption that follows it.
  5. Insurance cost and availability as the transmission channel to value.
  6. Black swan: a regional earthquake that removes liquidity and value at once.
  7. Black swan: tenant restructuring or bankruptcy.

Recommended sequence

  1. No listing and no renewal signature for 90 days.
  2. Assemble four documents: lease, depreciation schedules, lender statement, written objectives.
  3. Commission an appraisal and a structural and seismic report.
  4. Obtain a rent opinion with named comparables.
  5. Run both cases in the private calculator with real inputs.
  6. Set a reserve price, then decide.

What would change the answer

  • A below-market tenant renewal option — pushes toward selling now.
  • A very low basis with large recapture — pushes toward holding or restructuring.
  • A loan maturing before 2030 — the loan, not the lease, sets the deadline.
  • Any sign this store is on the closure list — sell in the next 24 months.
