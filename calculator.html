Runs entirely in your browser

This calculator makes no network requests of any kind. Nothing you type is transmitted, logged, saved or shared. Close the tab and it is gone. It applies only the tax rules that were verified from official IRS and California Franchise Tax Board publications; every rule it deliberately omits is listed below the result.

Rules this calculator does not apply

  • California alternative minimum tax — the threshold is applied as a warning only, because the computation needs Schedule P (540) preference items from your full return. TX-16
  • Property-tax reassessment on sale (Prop 13) — the 1.181% LA County FY24–25 average rate is verified (TX-11) and affects buyer underwriting rather than seller income tax directly.
  • FTB Form 593 closing withholding — displayed as an informational prepayment check (3⅓% gross or 12.3% individual gain election), not added onto final California tax. TX-12
  • Section 199A, Opportunity Zone provisions, itemised deductions, credits, IRC §453A installment interest, California Form 3840 out-of-state §1031 tracking, and any recapture beyond the 25% §1250 ceiling — not verified, so not applied.

The full machine-readable list of exclusions is in data/rules.json. Have a CPA review any number before it is used in a commitment.

Privacy. This site publishes only aggregate, non-identifying findings. No owner name, tenant contact, lease document, parcel identifier, loan figure or financial record appears here. Those belong in the owner's access-controlled internal workspace with authorised advisers.
