Verified federal and California rules reduced to one comparable number — with every omitted rule stated out loud.

The after-tax module turns verified rules into one number

The purpose of this page is narrow: take the rules that were read from official publications and reduce them to a single after-tax figure the owner can hold next to the hold case. It does not need the property to explain the rules, and it is explicit about which rules it refuses to apply because they could not be verified.

What this module deliberately does not do

Rules it applies, including the one that was hard to verify

  • California Behavioral Health Services Tax — verified from the FTB instructions (line 62) and applied: 1% of taxable income above $1,000,000. TX-07
  • Unrecaptured section 1250 gain — taxed at the lesser of the ordinary marginal rate and 25%, because the IRS describes 25% as a maximum rather than a flat rate. TX-03

Excluded rules — and the direction of the error

  • California alternative minimum tax — the threshold is published ($92,749 single or head of household, $123,667 joint, $61,830 separate) but the computation depends on Schedule P (540) preference items from the owner's full return, so it is not modelled. TX-16
  • Property-tax reassessment on sale (Prop 13) — the 1.181% LA County FY24–25 average rate is now verified (TX-11), and affects what a buyer can bid when NNN leases expire or cap reimbursements, though it is not a direct seller income tax.
  • FTB Form 593 closing withholding — now verified (TX-12: 3⅓% of sales price or 12.3% optional gain election). It is an escrow prepayment credited against your California income tax, displayed as an informational check in the calculator rather than added on top of final tax.
  • Section 199A and Opportunity Zone provisions, and itemised deductions, credits and exemption credits — not verified for this owner, so not applied.
  • Any depreciation recapture beyond the 25% unrecaptured §1250 ceiling — requires owner depreciation schedules (TX-13).

Every omission is listed in data/rules.json under excluded, so the gap travels with the code.

Suggested improvements

  • Confirm parcel municipal boundaries for Measure ULA — effective after June 30, 2026, City of LA sales above $5.4M trigger 4.0% (≥$10.9M: 5.5%) on gross consideration including liens, alongside the 0.56% City+County base tax.
  • Compare outright sale vs. installment sale vs. §1031 exchange directly in the private calculator once basis and depreciation schedules (GATE-03) are assembled.
  • Model California AMT (Schedule P 540) and Form 3840 out-of-state §1031 tracking with a CPA before any sale structure is locked in.
  • Verify the tenant's NNN property-tax pass-through against BOE Prop 13 reassessment rules (1.181% LA County avg) to gauge buyer underwriting sensitivity.
