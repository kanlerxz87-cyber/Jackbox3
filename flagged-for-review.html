Method irregularities found during the build

#IrregularityWhat was done about it
1api.census.gov rejected keyless requests with "A valid key must be included".ACS figures were read from the Census Reporter API mirror of the same acs2024_5yr release and labelled as such. An independent check on data.census.gov is requested.
2The BLS QCEW CSV endpoints returned HTTP 500, and the metro industry series IDs tried did not resolve.Industry-level employment was dropped rather than approximated. Only the series that responded are used.
3The BLS LAUS series reported October 2025 as unavailable due to the 2025 lapse in appropriations, and the 2025 annual average as an eleven-month figure.Recorded as a footnote on the affected row (EC-04) instead of being smoothed over.
4The USGS ASCE 7-16 service returned null for s1d, sd1, sm1 and sdc1 while populating sds and sdc.The nulls are published as nulls (H-08). They were not back-filled from a neighbouring grid point.
5The FEMA NRI WFIR_EVNTS field returned null for the tract.Recorded as unverified (H-16), not as zero.
6The CAL FIRE LRA layer returned no polygon at the reference point.A positive control at Griffith Park returned "Very High", proving the query works. The empty result is reported as indirect rather than as a clearance (H-03, H-04).
7The NCES school-district endpoint ignored the county filter and returned Alameda County districts with a statewide count.The count was discarded and recorded as unverified (SC-02).
8The California Franchise Tax Board 2025 Form 540 instructions did not load on the first two attempts.Retrieved on a later attempt. The 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax above $1,000,000 (line 62), the standard deduction chart (line 18) and the AMT thresholds (line 61) are all read from it, so the surcharge is applied, not excluded (TX-07, TX-15, TX-16).
9The Jack in the Box DEF 14A accession was filed 2025-12-30 but covers the fiscal year ended 2025-09-28, so filing dates and fiscal-year labels do not line up intuitively.The covered fiscal year is stated explicitly on every affected row.
10data.sec.gov XBRL Revenues returns restated values for the same period across different accessions.The FY2025 10-K value is used and its accession recorded (TE-02).
11Del Taco was sold on 22 December 2025 and moved to discontinued operations, so year-over-year revenue comparisons are not like for like.Stated on TE-03 and in the tenant-credit narrative.
12An 8-K under Item 5.02 was filed on 17 November 2026 and its body was not read.Recorded as flagged (TE-18) rather than assuming continuity of management.
13The parcel address is not in this repository, by design.All spatial analysis uses an anonymous Civic Center reference point, labelled as a proxy everywhere it appears.
14No market value, capitalisation rate, rent comparable, property-tax bill, insurance premium or mortgage balance could be obtained from an official source.All such cells are blank (VA-01 to VA-08). Private commercial data is not published by any official body, so more research cannot produce it; only the owner's documents can.

What would close each of these

  • A Census API key closes the ACS-mirror concern and unlocks the multi-year tract series.
  • A CPA closes the remaining tax-rule exclusions, including AMT and the property-tax reassessment on sale.
  • The parcel address closes every spatial caveat in one pass.
  • The four owner documents close the value, basis, loan and lease gaps.
  • A broker and an engineer close the rent, cap-rate and structural gaps.

Privacy. This site publishes only aggregate, non-identifying findings. No owner name, tenant contact, lease document, parcel identifier, loan figure or financial record appears here. Those belong in the owner's access-controlled internal workspace with authorised advisers.

Nature of this site. It is a research and decision-gate register. It is not an appraisal, a tax opinion, a legal opinion, or investment advice. A blank means "not verified", not "zero".
