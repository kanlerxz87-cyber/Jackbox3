Tenant & franchise credit

The tenant read from its own SEC filings: revenue, same-store sales, unit count and profitability, with the implications separated from the facts.

The tenant is the cash flow

Under a triple-net lease the owner receives rent and the tenant carries the operating risk. That makes the tenant's credit the owner's real asset. This page reads the tenant's own SEC filings rather than its marketing, and separates what the filings show from what they imply.

For the 40 weeks ended July 5, 2026 (S43 Q3 FY26 10-Q), system same-store sales ran −4.2%. Crucially for an owner under a triple-net or sublease structure, franchise variable/percentage rental income fell 18.0% YoY ($66.3m vs $80.8m) while occupancy costs stayed nearly flat (−2.3%), compressing JACK's net franchise rental spread by 15.4% ($76.5m vs $90.4m). Operating cash flow dropped to $15.0m (from $91.6m prior year) partly due to ~$11.0m of franchisee rent/royalty deferrals. Under Note 5, JACK's standard real estate leases carry 20-year initial terms plus 1–20 year sole-discretion tenant renewal options — underscoring why GATE-01 must inspect whether renewal options rest solely with the tenant.

What the filings show, stated carefully

Negative for the owner

  • FY2025 net loss of $80.7 million and 40-week operating cash flow drop to $15.0 million (after ~$11m franchisee deferrals).
  • 53 net closures and an explicit "JACK on Track" pruning programme; 41.3% of units sit in California facing $20/hr AB 1228 wages.
  • $1,492.0m securitized debt with $23.3m cash sweep (Senior ABS leverage >5.25×) and ARD maturities clustering in 2029–2032.
  • Residual overhangs: $(47.4)m Del Taco discontinued loss + March 2026 Delaware Chancery suit, $22.9m Qdoba lease guarantees, and 2026–27 C-suite turnover.

Neutral or positive

  • SSS decline narrowed sharply: −7.1% (Q3 FY25) to −1.1% (Q3 FY26).
  • June 2026 Series 2026-1 ($500m, 7.624%) refinancing completed, pushing that tranche ARD out to May 2031 and funding partial prepayment.
  • Active capital generation: $119.3m net Del Taco cash proceeds plus $18.9m YTD real estate sale gains used for deleveraging.
  • Quarterly adjusted EBITDA improved to $61.2 million from $57.1 million year over year.

What this means for the renewal case

A tenant with a shrinking unit base, compressed franchise rental spread, and 2029–2032 securitized debt ARDs has less strategic need for marginal sites and faces refinancing scrutiny inside the exact 2030–2035 window. Check whether the subject lease grants 1–20 year tenant-controlled extension options (Note 5) and obtain store-level sales and franchisee financials (GATE-08).

What is deliberately not asserted

No agency credit rating, default probability, or store-level unit P&L is asserted. Rating-agency publications remain gated behind paywalls/logins, so TE-16 stays blank. Subject lease economics and unit sales remain private owner/franchisee records (TE-19, TE-20).

Suggested improvements

  • Read the FY2026 10-K when it is filed and rebuild this page from audited annual figures rather than quarterly releases.
  • Add the franchisor's disclosure document (the Franchise Disclosure Document), which contains unit-level financial performance representations in Item 19 where the company elects to provide them.
  • Add the store-level profit and loss from the franchisee — the single most informative private document for this decision.
  • Track the closure programme by market in each quarterly release, since a closure announced in Los Angeles would be the earliest public signal of risk to this specific site.
